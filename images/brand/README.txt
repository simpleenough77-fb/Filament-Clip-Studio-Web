Palette: black #111111; accent blue #0954C4; gray #666666; white #FFFFFF.
Colors are clean approximations of the supplied raster reference.

Reconstruction: the large standalone CS mark is the geometric master. Its
rounded two-component shape is manually rebuilt with compact Bezier paths.
CLIP is custom rebuilt as outlines; STUDIO and supporting text are converted
from a closely matched Helvetica Neue outline, so they require no font.
Small variations in the reference's generated artwork are standardized across
assets. This is a visual reconstruction, not an original source-font recovery.

The maker's mark retains the approved master geometry. Fine features at 12 px
or embossed/debossed at small physical sizes should be tested in the intended
medium; SVG validation does not establish physical print performance.
