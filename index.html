YOUR FILAMENT, CLEARLY LABELED

Readable labels for every spool, even inside the AMS

Print label clips that fit onto your filament spools and show the brand, type and color in large, readable text. Holders mount on the front of your AMS units and dryers, so you can still see what’s loaded.

How it works

1 · PICK

Choose your filaments

Select brand, type and color from the catalog, import a CSV, or paste rows from a spreadsheet. Up to 100 clips per batch.

2 · PRINT

Download one 3MF

The studio lays out every clip on print plates for your printer (Bambu Lab, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic, Snapmaker or Prusa) and writes the file for your slicer. Enter your AMS and dryer counts to add the holders and mounting templates they need.

3 · CLIP

Label and mount

Clip a label onto each spool. When a spool goes into an AMS or dryer, its clip goes in the holder on the front, where a sleeve on the clip secures it.

Build it, print it, clip it on, and keep your filament always in view.

Supported spools

Each clip is shaped and physically tested for one brand’s spool, so support grows one fitted brand at a time. Sunlu’s 1 kg rolls and Jayo’s 1.1 kg reusable spools have their own fitted clips. For every supported brand, the catalog lists every public type and color the manufacturer shows on its website, with a link to the source.

Holders for your AMS and dryers

One-, two- and four-post holders (with a split four-post version for smaller printers) mount with 24 mm double-sided nano tape. Mounting templates center them on the Bambu Lab AMS 2 Pro, AMS HT and Creality SpacePi X4, and the clips stay put even with the lid fully open.
