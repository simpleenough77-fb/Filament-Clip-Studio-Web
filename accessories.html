Mounting a holder

You need the holder, the template for your device, 24 mm double-sided nano tape and isopropyl alcohol.

  1. Clean both surfaces, the spot on the device and the back of the holder, with isopropyl alcohol. Let them dry.
  2. Place the template on the device. It centers the holder on the device.
  3. Dry-fit first: set the holder in the template without tape and check the position.
  4. Apply the tape to the back of the holder.
  5. Using the template to position it, press the holder onto the device firmly, then remove the template.

Tape is hard to reposition once it has bonded, which is why the dry fit comes first.
