What is saved

Your rating, the topics you pick and your note, with the date it was sent. No account, email, IP address or browser details are stored. Responses are shown above as plain text, newest first, with no name or other details. Comments with links, contact details, abusive language or spam are held back until they have been reviewed, and anything abusive or personal is removed. Because it is anonymous, there is no way to reply. If something is broken or a spool is missing, the GitHub issue form lets you follow up.
